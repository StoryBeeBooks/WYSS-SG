What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your hard disk by a web page server when you visit a website. Cookies cannot be used to run programs or deliver viruses to your computer. Cookies are uniquely assigned to you, and can only be read by a web server in the domain that issued the cookie to you.

Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and provide information to website owners about how visitors use their sites.

How We Use Cookies

One of the primary purposes of cookies is to provide a convenience feature to save you time. The purpose of a cookie is to tell the web server that you have returned to a specific page. For example, if you personalize WYSS pages, or register with WYSS site or services, a cookie helps WYSS to recall your specific information on subsequent visits.

WYSS uses cookies to:

  • Help you personalize your online experience
  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Deliver customized content and advertising to customers whose behavior indicates interest in particular subject areas
  • Maintain website security and prevent fraud
  • Track the websites and pages our customers visit within WYSS to determine what services are most popular

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation, access to secure areas of the website, and remembering information you have entered into forms. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

Performance and Analytics Cookies: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This includes information about which pages are visited most often, how long visitors spend on each page, and any error messages received. This information helps us improve how our website works.

Functionality Cookies: These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your user name, language, or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features. They may also be used to provide services you have requested such as watching a video or commenting on content.

Targeting and Advertising Cookies: These cookies track your online activity to help deliver more relevant advertising or to limit how many times you see an advertisement. They are also used to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

Managing and Controlling Cookies

You have the ability to accept or decline cookies. Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline cookies if you prefer.

You can control and manage cookies in various ways:

  • Browser Controls: Most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies from websites through browser settings
  • Delete Cookies: You can delete cookies individually or all at once
  • Block Cookies: You can block cookies from specific websites or block all cookies
  • Third-Party Tools: Various third-party tools are available to manage cookies across different websites

If you choose to decline cookies, you may not be able to fully experience the interactive features of the WYSS services or websites you visit. Some features and services may not function properly without cookies.

How to Control Cookies in Your Browser

Instructions for managing cookies in popular web browsers:

Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data

Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data

Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data

Microsoft Edge: Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies and site permissions

Internet Explorer: Tools > Internet Options > Privacy > Advanced

For other browsers, please consult the browser's help documentation or visit the browser developer's website.

Cookie Duration

Cookies may be either "session" cookies or "persistent" cookies:

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain on your device until they expire or you delete them. The expiration period varies depending on the purpose of the cookie
