Accessibility

Our commitment to making our website accessible to all users.

Our Commitment

WYSS Industries is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Standards

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities and more user-friendly for everyone.

Accessibility Features

Our website includes the following accessibility features:

  • Clear and consistent navigation structure
  • Alternative text for images and graphics
  • Keyboard navigation support
  • Sufficient color contrast for readability
  • Responsive design for various devices and screen sizes
  • Descriptive link text
  • Properly structured headings
  • Form labels and error messages

Assistive Technology Compatibility

Our website is designed to be compatible with commonly used assistive technologies, including:

  • Screen readers
  • Speech recognition software
  • Text-to-speech tools
  • Screen magnification software
  • Alternative input devices

Ongoing Improvements

We recognize that accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly:

  • Review our website for accessibility issues
  • Update content and features to improve accessibility
  • Test our website with various assistive technologies
  • Train our team on accessibility best practices
  • Seek feedback from users with disabilities

Third-Party Content

While we strive to ensure that our website is accessible, some content may be provided by third parties. We work with our partners to ensure their content meets accessibility standards, but we may not have full control over third-party accessibility.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. Your input helps us identify areas for improvement and better serve all our users.
