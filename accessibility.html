Accessibility

Our commitment to making our website accessible to all users.

Our Commitment to Accessibility

WYSS is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to ensure our website is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability.

Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

The WYSS website is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard. We are actively working to achieve full conformance and regularly assess and update our website to improve accessibility.

Accessibility Features

We have implemented the following features to enhance accessibility:

  • Alternative text for images and graphics
  • Keyboard navigation support for all interactive elements
  • Clear and consistent navigation structure across all pages
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios for text and background colors
  • Resizable text without loss of functionality or content
  • Skip navigation links to bypass repetitive content
  • ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications) landmarks and labels
  • Properly structured headings for logical content hierarchy
  • Descriptive link text that makes sense out of context
  • Form labels and clear error messages
  • Responsive design that works across various devices and screen sizes
  • Video and audio content with captions and transcripts where applicable

Technical Specifications

Accessibility of the WYSS website relies on the following technologies to work with the particular combination of web browser and any assistive technologies or plugins installed on your computer:

  • HTML5 - for semantic markup and structure
  • CSS3 - for presentation and styling
  • JavaScript - for interactive functionality
  • WAI-ARIA - for enhanced accessibility information

These technologies are relied upon for conformance with the accessibility standards used.

Compatible Assistive Technologies

Our website is designed to be compatible with commonly used assistive technologies and adaptive strategies, including:

  • Screen readers (such as JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver)
  • Screen magnification software
  • Speech recognition software
  • Text-to-speech tools
  • Alternative input devices (such as switches, pointing devices)
  • Browser extensions and plugins for accessibility

Assessment Approach

WYSS assessed the accessibility of this website through the following methods:

  • Self-evaluation using automated accessibility testing tools
  • Manual testing with various assistive technologies
  • Ongoing monitoring and regular accessibility audits
  • Testing with keyboard-only navigation
  • Color contrast analysis
  • Review of WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance

Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts to ensure accessibility of the WYSS website, there may be some limitations. We are aware of the following:

  • Some legacy PDF documents may not be fully accessible
  • Third-party embedded content may not meet our accessibility standards
  • Some complex interactive features are still being optimized for full accessibility

We are actively working to address these limitations and welcome your feedback on any accessibility barriers you encounter.

Feedback and Contact Information

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the WYSS website. Please let us know if you encounter accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement:

Please visit our Contact Us page to get in touch with us regarding accessibility concerns.

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days.

When contacting us about accessibility, please provide:

  • The specific webpage URL where you experienced the issue
  • A description of the accessibility barrier or problem
  • Information about the assistive technology you are using (if applicable)
  • Your contact information so we can follow up with you

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We continuously work to improve the accessibility of our website by:

  • Conducting regular accessibility reviews and audits
  • Updating content and features to improve accessibility
  • Testing our website with various assistive technologies and browsers
  • Training our team on accessibility best practices and guidelines
  • Seeking feedback from users with disabilities
  • Staying informed about new accessibility standards and requirements
  • Prioritizing accessibility in all new development and design work

Third-Party Content

While we strive to ensure that our website is fully accessible, some content may be provided by third parties. We work with our partners and vendors to ensure their content meets accessibility standards, but we may not have full control over the accessibility of all third-party content. If you encounter accessibility issues with third-party content on our site, please let us know.

Alternative Formats

If you require website content in an alternative format (such as large print, audio, or accessible electronic format), please contact us. We will work to accommodate your request in a timely manner.

Compliance with Singapore Standards

In addition to international WCAG standards, we strive to align with Singapore's accessibility guidelines and best practices to ensure our website serves the needs of all users in Singapore and the wider region.
