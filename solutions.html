Regional Energy Centers & Distributed Generation

Regional Energy Centers represent a paradigm of centralized energy infrastructure, leveraging the principles of distributed generation and Combined Cooling, Heating, and Power (CCHP) to serve large-scale developments such as medical campuses, business districts, and transportation hubs.

Key Principles & Applications

By centralizing the production of thermal and electrical energy, these facilities maximize the efficiency of primary fuel sources, achieving overall energy utilization rates exceeding 80%. We design and integrate CCHP systems that cover the composite energy demands of an entire district—from HVAC cooling and heating to domestic hot water and backup power—all from a single, highly optimized plant.

Technical Advantages

  • Enhanced Thermodynamic Efficiency: Significantly reduces primary energy consumption and carbon emissions compared to decentralized systems.
  • Operational Resilience: Provides a stable and reliable source of power and thermal energy, crucial for critical facilities.
  • Scalability & Flexibility: Engineered to meet fluctuating load demands across multiple end-users with varying consumption profiles.

Ice Thermal Storage (TES) for Peak Load Management

Ice Thermal Storage is a strategic energy load management solution designed to decouple cooling demand from peak electricity consumption periods. By shifting the electrical load to off-peak hours—when electricity tariffs are lowest—this technology significantly reduces operational expenditures (OPEX) and lessens the strain on the public power grid.

Key Principles & Applications

The system utilizes chillers during the night to generate and store thermal energy in the form of ice. During peak demand hours the following day, the stored ice is melted to provide chilled water for the facility's cooling needs, reducing or eliminating the need to run chillers. We engineer both Internal Melt and External Melt systems, selecting the optimal configuration based on the project's load profile, spatial constraints, and performance requirements.

Technical Advantages

  • Significant OPEX Reduction: Leverages time-of-use electricity pricing for substantial cost savings.
  • Peak Demand Shaving: Reduces a facility's peak electrical demand, potentially lowering capacity charges from utility providers.
  • Increased System Resiliency: Provides a buffer of stored cooling capacity that can be dispatched during power outages or chiller downtime.
  • Reduced Chiller Plant Size: Can allow for smaller chiller installations, reducing capital expenditures (CAPEX).
Project Highlight: Suzhou Olympic Sports Arena

For this 360,000 m² first-class stadium, we engineered an Internal Melt system with a capacity of 13,650 RTH. The system is designed for dynamic load-shifting to accommodate the variable cooling requirements of large-scale sporting and entertainment events with maximum economic efficiency.

Chill Supply Chain & Precision Temperature Control

Our Chill Supply Chain solutions are engineered to provide precise, stable, and reliable low-temperature environments essential for the preservation of temperature-sensitive goods. These are not merely cooling systems; they are mission-critical infrastructures where even minor deviations can result in significant product and financial loss.

Key Principles & Applications

We design and construct facilities for two primary temperature ranges: Cold Storage (0°C to +5°C) for fresh products like fruit and beverages, and Refrigerate Warehouses (-15°C to -21°C) for frozen goods such as meat and aquatic products. The core of our solution is an integrated computer control and management system that provides robust monitoring, precise temperature regulation, and complete traceability of cargo conditions.

Technical Advantages

  • Guaranteed Product Integrity: Maintains exact temperature and humidity parameters to preserve the quality and safety of stored goods.
  • Advanced Control & Monitoring: Implements sophisticated management systems to track cargo origin, monitor environmental conditions in real-time, and minimize loss.
  • High Reliability & Redundancy: Systems are designed with fail-safes and redundancy to ensure continuous operation, protecting high-value inventory.
  • Customized Engineering: Solutions are tailored to the specific thermal properties and storage requirements of the target cargo.

Proactive & Preventative Maintenance

Preventative maintenance is a foundational engineering practice designed to mitigate the risk of unplanned failures and combat the inevitable degradation of system performance over time.

Engineering Principle

Mechanical equipment, particularly heat exchangers like cooling towers, is subject to performance decay from environmental factors. Airborne and waterborne impurities deposit on heat transfer surfaces (e.g., PVC fill, coils), which increases thermal resistance and compromises the design heat exchange ratio. This fouling forces other system components, such as compressors, to operate under higher loads, leading to increased energy consumption and accelerated wear. Our programs are designed to systematically counteract these effects.

Our Approach

  • Customized Inspection Protocols: We develop routine inspection and maintenance plans tailored to the specific equipment models, operational loads, and environmental conditions of each site.
  • System-Wide Analysis: Our service extends beyond individual components to include the entire hydronic and control system, ensuring all hardware (HVAC, boilers, pumps) and software (automation controls) function cohesively.
  • Focus on Core Performance: We prioritize the maintenance of heat exchange surfaces and mechanical integrity to ensure the entire cooling or heating system operates at its optimal design efficiency.
