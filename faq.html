Category 1: Engineering & Energy Solutions

What are Shanghai Liangling's primary engineering qualifications?

Shanghai Liangling M&E Installation Co., Ltd. holds a Certificate of Electronic & Intelligentialize Engineering Contractor (II) and an Eletromechanical Installation Contractor (I).

What is the core value of your project engineering?

Our core value is "Green energy saving & renewable resources technologies," aiming to be an expert in energy solutions.

What is a key technology you use for regional energy centers?

We utilize Combined Cooling, Heating, and Power (CCHP) triple supply systems, also known as distributed energy stations.

What is the energy utilization efficiency of your CCHP systems?

Our CCHP systems, like the one at Hongqiao International Medical Center, can achieve an energy utilization efficiency of over 80%.

What is the maximum cooling load of the Hongqiao International Medical Center's distributed power station?

The maximum cooling load is 68.907 MW per hour, and the maximum thermal load is 38.3101 MW per hour.

What types of ice thermal storage systems do you install?

We install both Internal Melt Ice Thermal Storage and External Melt Ice Thermal Storage systems.

What was the total ice storage capacity for the Suzhou Olympic Sports Center project?

The total ice storage capacity for the Suzhou Olympic Sports Center was 13,650 RTH (Refrigeration Ton-Hours).

What types of energy sources do you integrate into your multi-energy complementary systems?

We integrate waste heat steam from power plants, natural gas, electricity, and ice storage to improve energy efficiency.

What is the design cooling capacity of the Waigaoqiao Shenergy Power Center Station?

The general design cooling capacity is 64,397 kW and the thermal capacity is 31,997 kW.

Do you offer solutions for low-temperature supply chains?

Yes, we provide solutions for chill supply chains, including cold storage (0°C to +5°C) and refrigerated warehouses (-15°C to -21°C).

BAC's main products include evaporative condensers, closed-circuit cooling towers, and ice thermal storage equipment.

What technology is used in the WYSS Snow Dome machines?

The project utilizes the WYSS Third-generation patented ice crushing technology, specifically the "ICS system-CC20 series" snow making machine.

A single machine can make up to 43 cubic meters of snow per day.

What types of solar power generation systems do you install?

We install photovoltaic power generation systems consisting of solar panels, controllers, and inverters for industrial and commercial fields.

What is solar energy storage?

Solar energy storage involves storing additional electrical energy from solar panels in storage devices when sunlight is sufficient, for use when solar power is less than the system load.

What are the operating temperature ranges for your cold storage solutions?

We design for cold storage (0°C to +5°C) for fresh produce and refrigerated warehouses (-15°C to -21°C) for meat and aquatic products.
