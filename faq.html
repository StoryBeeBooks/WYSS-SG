Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about WYSS

General Questions

What is WYSS Industries?

WYSS Industries is a global, diversified manufacturer of industrial components for rail, commercial vehicle, automotive, and construction and building applications. We have over a century of engineering and manufacturing excellence.

Where are you located?

We have manufacturing facilities and offices across North America, Europe, and Asia. Our headquarters is located in the United States, and we serve customers worldwide through our global network.

What makes WYSS different from competitors?

As an employee-owned company, our workforce is directly invested in our success. This unique structure creates a culture of accountability, excellence, and long-term thinking. Combined with our century of experience and commitment to innovation, we deliver exceptional value to our customers.

Products & Services

What products do you manufacture?

We manufacture industrial components across multiple sectors including rail components (brake systems, suspension), commercial vehicle parts, automotive components, and construction materials. Visit our Products page for detailed information.

Do you offer custom manufacturing?

Yes! We offer custom manufacturing services for clients with unique requirements. Our engineering team works closely with customers to develop, prototype, and manufacture custom components tailored to specific needs.

What quality certifications do you have?

We maintain ISO certifications and comply with industry-specific standards across all our product lines. All our products are manufactured to the highest quality standards and backed by relevant industry certifications.

Do you provide technical support?

Yes, our expert technical support team is available to assist with product selection, installation guidance, troubleshooting, and ongoing maintenance. We provide comprehensive consultation services to help optimize your operations.

Orders & Delivery

How do I place an order?

Please contact our sales team through our Contact page or email us at info@wysstech.com. Our team will assist you with product selection, pricing, and order placement.

What are your lead times?

Lead times vary depending on the product and order quantity. Our sales team will provide specific delivery timeframes when you request a quote. We work to accommodate urgent requirements whenever possible.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we serve customers worldwide through our global network of facilities and distribution partners. We can arrange international shipping and handle export documentation as needed.

Sustainability & ESG

What is WYSS doing about sustainability?

We are committed to environmental stewardship, social responsibility, and strong governance. We have established measurable ESG goals including reducing greenhouse gas emissions, achieving zero waste to landfill, and increasing renewable energy use. Visit our ESG page for detailed information.

Do you publish sustainability reports?

Yes, we publish an annual ESG report detailing our performance, initiatives, and progress toward our goals. Our reporting follows recognized frameworks including GRI Standards, SASB, and TCFD.
