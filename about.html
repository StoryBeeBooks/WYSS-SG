About WYSS

Performance. Purpose. People.

Who We Are

WYSS is the operating brand of Shanghai Liangling Mechanical & Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd. (良菱机电设备成套有限公司). Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Shanghai, the company is a recognized specialist in energy solutions for commercial, industrial and large-scale landmark projects.

Our core focus is practical, high-performance engineering that delivers measurable energy savings and reliable operation across applications such as supertall buildings, data & cloud computing centers, industrial facilities, and cold-chain projects.

Vision

To be a trusted partner for mission-critical energy systems — designing and delivering solutions that reduce energy use, improve resilience, and lower total cost of ownership for our customers.

Strategy

We pursue a pragmatic strategy that combines proven international equipment, advanced control technologies, and local engineering excellence:

  • System-level thinking: design solutions that optimize the whole facility — not just individual components.
  • Trusted partnerships: source best‑in‑class equipment from established global manufacturers and integrate them with high-quality installation and commissioning.
  • Sustainability by design: prioritize energy-saving architectures such as thermal energy storage, efficient chillers and optimized pumping systems.
  • Lifecycle support: provide commissioning, preventive maintenance, parts supply and contract energy management to protect long-term performance.

Capabilities & Services

Our services cover the full project lifecycle:

  • Consulting and system design for HVAC and integrated energy centers (including ice-storage and tri-generation schemes).
  • Supply and installation of cooling towers, chillers, pumps and related equipment.
  • Commissioning, testing and performance verification on large and complex projects.
  • Operation, maintenance and facilities management with 24/7 technical support.
  • Contract energy management and performance-based service models.

We have long-term partnerships with international manufacturers and suppliers, and supply products such as BAC cooling towers, Grundfos pumps, GEA equipment, Danfoss controls and York systems.

Sustainability & Quality

Energy efficiency is central to our projects. We design solutions to minimize operating energy and maximize lifecycle value, applying industry standards and rigorous quality control throughout procurement, installation and commissioning.

Experience & Team

Shanghai Liangling has decades of experience in the Yangtze River Delta HVAC market, delivering projects for supertall buildings, data centers and industrial clients. The company holds professional qualifications for mechanical and electrical installation and maintains an experienced team of engineers and project managers to ensure professional execution.
