About WYSS

Performance. Purpose. People.

Our Story

WYSS Industries has been at the forefront of industrial manufacturing for over a century. Founded on principles of quality, innovation, and customer service, we've grown from a small manufacturing operation to a global leader in industrial components.

Throughout our history, we've remained committed to the values that made us successful: integrity, excellence, and a dedication to continuous improvement.

Our Mission

We're on a mission to deliver premium value to our customers for the betterment of our employees and communities. As an employee-owned company, our global workforce is united in their passion for leadership and excellence.

Every day, we strive to exceed expectations, push the boundaries of what's possible, and create lasting value for all our stakeholders.

Our Values

Performance: We deliver exceptional results through continuous improvement and operational excellence.

Purpose: We're driven by a commitment to make a positive impact on our customers, employees, and communities.

People: Our employee-owners are our greatest asset, and we invest in their growth and success.

Global Presence

With manufacturing facilities and offices across North America, Europe, and Asia, WYSS serves customers worldwide. Our global footprint enables us to provide local support with the resources and capabilities of a multinational corporation.

We employ thousands of skilled professionals who are passionate about delivering quality products and exceptional service.

Innovation & Technology

Innovation is in our DNA. We invest heavily in research and development, advanced manufacturing technologies, and continuous process improvement. Our state-of-the-art facilities incorporate the latest in automation, quality control, and sustainable manufacturing practices.

Employee Ownership

As an employee-owned company, every member of our team has a stake in our success. This unique structure aligns our interests with those of our customers and creates a culture of accountability, excellence, and long-term thinking.
