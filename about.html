Shanghai Liangling M&E Equipment Co., Ltd.

WYSS is the operating brand of Shanghai Liangling Mechanical & Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd. Established in 1999, we have grown to become a nationwide leader in HVAC solutions with registered capital of 30 million yuan and a team of over 120 employees, including 40 professional technicians.

Headquartered in Shanghai—the heart of China's economic landscape—we maintain a fully-developed network of sales and service offices in key cities including Suzhou, Hangzhou, Nanjing, Guangzhou, Chongqing, Chengdu, and Beijing, ensuring comprehensive coverage and support across the nation.

Comprehensive HVAC Solutions

Whether serving commercial or industrial demands, public or civil constructions, we proudly offer complete HVAC equipment solutions and professional installation services. Our expertise extends to automatic control systems for air conditioning, seamlessly combining cutting-edge technology with premium products.

As specialists and major players in energy management, terminal system design, maintenance, and implementation, we have earned our reputation as experts in HVAC Energy Programs. Our core value of "Green energy saving & renewable resources technologies" drives every project, positioning us as pioneers in the energy management business.

Building Excellence Through Trusted Global Alliances

Leading Partnerships in the Asia-Pacific Region

Shanghai Liangling M&E Equipment Co., Ltd. has established itself as a premier distributor of world-class HVAC solutions. For over 20 years, we have been the largest authorized distributor of BAC (Baltimore Aircoil Company) in the Asia-Pacific region—a testament to our commitment to excellence and trusted partnership.

Beyond our flagship BAC partnership, we maintain long-term collaborations with leading multinational brands including GRUNDFOS, GEA, Danfoss, WYSS, YORK, and Johnson Controls. These strategic alliances enable us to deliver comprehensive, best-in-class solutions to our clients across China.

The BAC Partnership: Two Decades of Trust & Growth

Baltimore Aircoil Company established BAC Dalian Co., Ltd. in 1997 as a joint venture with Dalian Bingshan Group, producing evaporative condensers, closed-circuit cooling towers, and ice thermal storage equipment. In 2010, BAC founded BAC Suzhou Co., Ltd. as a wholly-owned subsidiary focused on open-circuit cooling towers. Both facilities merged in 2019 to form the unified BAC China company.

Our 20-year collaboration with BAC is built on absolute trust and shared development goals. As the exclusive agency for BAC in the eastern, southern, and southwestern regions of China, and the largest agency in the entire Asia-Pacific region, we have established an unparalleled distribution network and technical support infrastructure.
