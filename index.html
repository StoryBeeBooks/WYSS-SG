Across Industries

WYSS is the operating brand of Shanghai Liangling Mechanical & Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd. Established in 1999, Shanghai Liangling M&E Equipment Co., Ltd. has registered capital of 30 million yuan and over 120 employees, including 40 professional technicians. Whether it is commercial or industrial demand, public or civil constructions, we are proud to offer all kinds of HVAC equipment and installation works. We also provide automatic control for air conditioning systems, combining technology with premium products to deliver excellence across all markets we serve.

Engineering Excellence Through Advanced Solutions

We deliver specialized engineering solutions for the most demanding energy and environmental challenges. Our core competencies encompass the design and implementation of large-scale Regional Energy Centers for optimized CCHP efficiency, the deployment of Ice Thermal Storage systems for strategic peak load management, and the construction of mission-critical Chill Supply Chain facilities.
