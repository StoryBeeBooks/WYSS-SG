BAC Heat Rejection & Thermal Management Systems

Largest Authorized Distributor - Asia-Pacific

As the largest authorized distributor for Baltimore Aircoil Company (BAC) in the Asia-Pacific region, we provide a comprehensive suite of solutions for heat rejection and thermal storage.

01

Open Circuit Cooling Towers

Engineering Principle

These towers utilize the highly efficient principle of direct-contact evaporative cooling, where process water is distributed over a fill media, maximizing air-water interface to achieve superior heat rejection through latent heat of vaporization.

Applications

Ideal for standard HVAC comfort cooling and a wide range of industrial processes requiring a robust, cost-effective, and high-efficiency heat rejection solution with a wide range of water flow rates.

02

Closed Circuit Cooling Towers

Engineering Principle

Employs a clean, closed-loop coil to isolate the process fluid (e.g., water, glycol solution, or oil) from the external environment. Evaporative cooling is applied to the outside of the coil, providing indirect but highly effective heat rejection without introducing airborne contaminants or requiring fluid filtration.

Applications

Critical for applications where process fluid purity is paramount, such as data center cooling, industrial processes with sensitive equipment (compressors, transformers), and food-grade manufacturing.

03

Ice Storage Products (Thermal Energy Storage)

Engineering Principle

Leverages the high latent heat of fusion of water (334 kJ/kg) to store thermal energy. BAC's exclusive coil technology optimizes ice formation and melting cycles, providing consistent outlet temperatures (2.2-3.3°C for inner melt, 0-1.1°C for outer melt). Coils are constructed of hot-dip galvanized or stainless steel for a 20-year design life.

Applications

A strategic energy management solution for reducing peak electrical demand, shifting energy consumption to off-peak hours (load-shifting), and reducing the capital cost of the primary chiller plant.

04

Evaporative Condensers

Engineering Principle

Functions as a direct heat exchanger within a refrigeration cycle, rejecting heat from the high-pressure refrigerant gas by evaporating water over the condensing coil. This process achieves significantly lower condensing temperatures (8-11°C less than air-cooled systems), which reduces compressor lift and can lower system horsepower consumption by up to 30%.

Applications

An integral component for high-efficiency industrial refrigeration and large-tonnage air-conditioning systems where reducing compressor energy consumption is a primary objective.

05

HXV B Hybrid Coolers

Engineering Principle

Utilizes a patented Combined Operation Technology (COT) that intelligently integrates evaporative (latent heat) and dry cooling (sensible heat) modes. This hybrid approach allows for operation in full dry mode, full evaporative mode, or adiabatic mode for optimized performance based on ambient conditions and operational needs.

Applications

The definitive solution for challenging projects where water conservation is critical (up to 70% reduction), plume abatement is required (airports, urban centers), or adherence to stringent energy standards like ASHRAE 90.1 is mandatory.
